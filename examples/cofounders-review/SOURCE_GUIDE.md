2. Compare the exact passages below. Check the [review guide](REVIEW_GUIDE.md) for adverse elements, missing evidence and questions that remain open.

For S06, read the changed approval rule **together with the retained operational coordination**. The excerpt does not supply transaction-level commitment dates or approvals. It cannot, by itself, allocate responsibility or establish an economic loss.
